ABOUT

I learn by shipping, not just studying.

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student at Raj Kumar Goel Institute of Technology (RKGIT), AKTU, and most of what I know about backend engineering came from building and breaking my own applications in production — not from a syllabus.

My work centers on Python and Flask: designing REST APIs, modeling data in PostgreSQL and MongoDB, and hardening applications after they're live — things like connection leaks, race conditions, and authentication gaps that only show up under real usage.

I haven't done a formal internship yet, so my two deployed projects, InstantShare and Expense Tracker, are where I've actually learned to debug production systems rather than toy ones.
